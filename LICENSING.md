## Publication-layer data treatment

The public repository omits raw Baa data and explicit spread levels. It retains the project’s calculated indicators and authored analysis, with source attribution. Raw Baa data and explicit Baa spread-level observations or benchmark levels are omitted from the public package. Original code, methodology, schemas, credit labels, derived stress/frequency outputs and authored credit watchlist remain included. The complete local analysis retains its full data. This publication treatment does not grant a new third-party data license and does not change the financial methodology.
